assistant: Yes, you can. SubViewport renders to a texture — that texture gets displayed on a 3D surface (like a `QuadMesh`). So:

```
Node3D (can rotate/move in 3D space)
└── SubViewport (renders 2D content to texture)
    └── 2D scene (MarginContainer, FrameContainer, etc.)

QuadMesh (displays the SubViewport texture in 3D world)
```

The SubViewport itself stays 2D, but its parent Node3D handles the 3D transform. The texture output is what's placed in 3D space.

**So you don't need Page to BE Node3D** — just parent the SubViewport to a Node3D and render it on a 3D quad.
